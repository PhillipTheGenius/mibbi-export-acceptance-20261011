Customer-owned Supabase integration

This export uses the connected Supabase project's publishable key. It contains no management tokens or secret keys.
Serve this export over HTTPS. Do not open it as file:// for database features.
Customer authentication, records and private files remain in Supabase; they are not copied into this ZIP.
SQL is generated from the approved plan; take backups before applying changes. Mibbi never generates destructive table/column changes.
Each customer sees/edits their own private records. Public tables allow public reads. All writes require login.
Private uploads support JPEG, PNG, WebP and PDF up to 5 MB. Configure Auth email confirmations and SMTP in Supabase.
